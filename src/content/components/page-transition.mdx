A covering `div` that fades the canvas in and out when the visitor follows an internal link. Wired into `Layout` and `PlainLayout`. It does not enable Astro's client router — the next page still loads normally; the overlay just hides the swap.

## Behavior

- Intercepts same-origin `<a>` clicks (capture phase), fades the light canvas overlay in, then assigns the new URL.
- Overlay is pinned to `data-theme="light"` so the fade is always the interior canvas, even when leaving a dark page.
- Browser chrome (`theme-color`) and the document root are forced to the light canvas before and after the fade so Chrome does not flash black in the MPA swap gap.
- A head snippet (`PageTransitionHead`) marks the destination covered and paints the light canvas before first paint.
- Links marked `data-page-transition-kind="palm"` (homepage hero → work): fade to white, reveal the centered palm mark, navigate under that cover, wait for above-the-fold images on the destination, then fade the whole overlay out.
- The destination stays covered until above-the-fold images have decoded, then fades out. The intro loader is skipped on that arrival so the two overlays do not stack.
- Skips modifier-key clicks, new tabs, downloads, hashes, `mailto` / `tel`, and links marked `data-no-transition`.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` skips the overlay and navigates immediately.
- Without JS, a `<noscript>` rule hides the overlay so the page stays usable.
