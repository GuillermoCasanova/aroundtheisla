About

lara torruella

Photographer and art director focused on coastlines, light, and the slow rhythm of island life.

Born in San Juan and based between the north shore and the city, Lara works with brands, magazines, and small crews who care about place as much as product.

Her practice sits at the intersection of documentary travel and quiet fashion — long shadows, salt air, and frames that let the land lead the story.

Recent collaborators include regional hospitality groups, independent publishers, and the Around The Isla editorial platform.
